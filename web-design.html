SERVICE MENU

Services and options

Choose the services that fit your project. We’ll confirm the final scope with you before work begins.

Website services

SERVICE

Essential showcase website

Three pages to present your business, services, and contact details. We adapt an existing layout to your brand, add a form, and publish the site.

SERVICE

Signature showcase website

A five-page website with a design proposal for the homepage, a form, and basic settings that help search engines read your pages. We test the site and show you how to use it in a 45-minute session.

SERVICE

Online booking setup and integration

We configure your services, their durations, and your availability. We add the calendar to your website and test the booking process. Deposit setup is quoted separately. Booking-tool and payment fees are also separate.

Branding and design

BRANDING

Graphic design

A visual created from your current brand identity and prepared in two sizes. One round of revisions is included.

BRANDING

Essential logo

One logo concept based on your brand name. You receive a light version, a dark version, and ready-to-use files.

BRANDING

Visual identity

We present two starting concepts, then develop the one you choose. You receive the logo, colors, fonts, a usage guide, and three social media templates.

Optional services

OPTION

Extra page using an existing layout

We add a page using a layout already used on your site. You provide the content to include.

OPTION

Extra page with a new layout

We create a layout suited to the new page’s content, then add it to your site.

OPTION

Short-page copywriting

We write the text for a short page based on the information you provide.

OPTION

Online deposit setup

We set the amount due at booking and test the payment process. The scope depends on the tool and the rules to configure.

You provide the text and images unless your quote says otherwise. Two rounds of revisions are included unless noted differently. Domain, hosting, and external-tool costs are listed separately in your quote.
